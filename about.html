About Mize مايز

Built on expertise. Driven by trust.

Mize Auto Care was founded on a simple idea: drivers deserve workshop-grade repairs explained in plain language, with pricing they can rely on.

Our story

What started as a two-bay garage grew into a full-service workshop because our customers kept sending their friends and family. Every year we've reinvested in better tools, better training, and a cleaner space — but the philosophy hasn't changed: diagnose carefully, repair correctly, explain clearly.

Today, Mize serves thousands of drivers across the region with the same hands-on care we started with.

What we stand for

  • Integrity. If you don't need it, we won't sell it.
  • Craft. Every repair is done the way we'd do it for family.
  • Clarity. Written estimates, photo reports, real conversations.
  • Respect. For your time, your vehicle, and your budget.
